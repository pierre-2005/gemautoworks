# GEM Autoworks design

An automotive editorial: ivory #f5f5f1, carbon #14191c and original electric blue #1264df. Original GEM logo remains intact.

Schibsted Grotesk for display and body. Regular display weight, tracking at least -0.04em. 1280px container, 64px desktop and 24px phone gutters. Open layouts, 4px action corners, 12px form surfaces. Header and footer use carbon; one blue closing section carries the primary action.

Primary action: “Book an appointment”. One 24px SVG arrow system. Theme focus, selection, caret, hover, loading, empty, success and error states.

The user's explicit 3D direction leads the opening: a locally hosted CC BY 4.0 concept supercar, native-scroll orbit, opening doors, and paint choices. No scroll hijacking. Render on demand, cap device pixel ratio and suspend work when hidden. Reduced motion holds the car at its finished initial angle; WebGL and reduced-data fallbacks show a photograph. Keep the booking action visible without scrolling through the experience.

Once-only Taste hero entrance and one word-highlight statement. No repeated reveals, unrelated shaders, autoplay, fake figures or proof. The 3D brief overrides the initial photography-only hero deal.

Gallery uses a native, keyboard-operable image dialog. Booking preserves server validation and adds a live summary, linked time errors, fetch cancellation and submission feedback. Mobile navigation supports Escape, outside click and focus containment.

Editorial photographs: Unsplash photo-1503376780353-7e6692767b70 and photo-1580273916550-e323be2ae537. Original showcase remains CMS-managed. Stock is never added as customer work. 3D asset source, author and adaptation details are in static/licenses/3d-model.txt.
