Privacy policy

What GEM Autoworks LLC keeps when you use this site, and why.

What we collect

When you book, sign up or send a message we keep your name, email, phone number, the cars you add, the addresses you give for mobile visits, and your booking history. We also keep the reviews and messages you send.

Why

Only to schedule and carry out your details, confirm and remind you about visits, answer your messages and show the reviews you agreed to publish. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

On GEM Autoworks’ own server. Emails are sent through our email provider. The site sets one cookie to keep you signed in and to protect forms; it does not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Your choices

You can edit your details, cars and addresses in your account at any time. To have your account and history deleted, email {{ site.email }}.
