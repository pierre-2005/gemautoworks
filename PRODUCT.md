# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- **New local customers** in Central Massachusetts, mostly on phones, arriving from Instagram or search. Job: decide fast whether GEM is trustworthy and affordable, then book a time.
- **Repeat customers.** Job: rebook a saved car in seconds, and keep a car clean on a schedule without re-entering details.
- **Pavly, the owner** (23, runs GEM himself). Job: see today's jobs and addresses on his phone, confirm requests, set prices, hours and photos.

## Product Purpose
Turn GEM's Instagram-driven interest into booked, repeat detailing work, and give the owner one place to run bookings, customers and recurring plans. Success: bookings and plans made online without back-and-forth DMs.

## Positioning
A one-person mobile detailer with real before/after work, clear size-based prices and real open times, plus a garage and plan system that most local detailers do not offer.

## Operating Context
Mobile detailing at the customer's home or work, and optionally drop-off at a shop (admin switch: mobile, shop or both). One detailer, one calendar; travel time between jobs. Self-hosted on a Raspberry Pi (Docker, Portainer); email through SMTP; no online payment.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Packages (from GEM's Canva site): Exterior Detail $89/$99/$109, Interior Detail $119/$139/$159, GEM Detail $179/$199/$219 by sedan/SUV/truck. "Pricing may vary based on car condition and distance to customer."
- Offer: 10% off the first detail.
- Undecided, set by the owner in the admin: real weekly hours, job durations, shop address, towns covered.

## Brand Commitments
- Name "GEM Autoworks" / GEM Autoworks LLC; tagline "Your Car. Our Craft."; secondary line "Passion Drives Detail."
- The chrome-and-blue diamond logo, designed for dark grounds; never redrawn or recoloured.

## Evidence on Hand
- Photos from GEM's Instagram (assets-src/): Kia K5, Nissan Altima and console (before/after), Ford F-150 (before/after), Infiniti G37, Jeep Grand Cherokee; owner photo with a McLaren Senna (from the v1 site).
- Owner bio (Instagram "About me" post and Canva site).
- No customer reviews yet; none may be invented.

## Product Principles
1. Show the real work and the real price before asking for anything.
2. A booking is never more than five clear steps, and works without an account.
3. Everything the owner might change later (mode, prices, hours, copy, offer) is a setting, not code.
4. The owner's phone is the primary admin device.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, full keyboard use, visible labels and errors on every form, reduced-motion support.
